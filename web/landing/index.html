Architecture & Engineering

The Complete 8-Stage Memory Lifecycle

Most apps stop at note-taking. NYABAGAM forms a continuous, self-reinforcing memory feedback loop.

1
STAGE ONE

Multi-Modal Capture

Floating Capture Dock accessible anywhere, voice audio memos with Whisper transcription, or rapid text thoughts.

2
STAGE TWO

AI Understanding

Zero-hallucination Named Entity Recognition extracting People, Organizations, Things, Dates, and Amounts.

3
STAGE THREE

Candidate Remember

Transparent candidate verification. Review, modify, or approve memory candidates before committing to storage.

4
STAGE FOUR

Grounded Ask

Conversational Q&A backed by hybrid keyword + 1536-dim vector search with direct, verifiable evidence citations.

5
STAGE FIVE

Context Bridge

Proactive relevance engine connecting immediate situations ("AC isn't cooling") to past memories ("Ravi serviced it on Aug 18").

6
STAGE SIX

Action Approval

Strict human-in-the-loop guardrails. NYABAGAM pre-drafts WhatsApp/SMS messages but NEVER dispatches without your explicit tap.

7
STAGE SEVEN

Outcome Resolution

Tracks state transitions (e.g. appliance status: needs_service → active) while maintaining an immutable historical audit log.

8
STAGE EIGHT

Knowledge Graph

Continual relational graph enrichment linking people, things, and life events into a coherent, permanent personal memory network.

Core Features

Engineered for Your Actual Life

Built with uncompromising standards: liquid glass beauty, lightning performance, and absolute privacy.

Floating Capture Dock

Friction is the enemy of memory. The Floating Capture Dock hovers unobtrusively, letting you record a 5-second voice memo or tap a quick thought before the moment vanishes from your head.

2-Day Early Reminders

Zero AI Training Retention

Every serverless AI invocation enforces store: false. Your personal life, receipts, and health discussions are never retained or used to train OpenAI foundation models.

Privacy By Design

Our Sacred Privacy Commitments

Your memories belong to you. Not to an AI training cluster. Not to an advertising broker.

● ROW-LEVEL SECURITY

PostgreSQL User-Scoped RLS

Every database table strictly enforces auth.uid() = user_id at the engine level. No cross-tenant data leakage is physically possible.

● ZERO CLIENT SECRETS

Secure Serverless Boundary

Zero third-party API keys ever touch the mobile app client. All inference is brokered by authenticated serverless functions behind strict rate limits.

● EXPLICIT AUTHORIZATION

Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard

NYABAGAM will never automatically message, call, or email anyone. Action sheets present transparent drafts that you must tap to approve.

● ZERO-TRAINING RETENTION

Zero-Retention AI

OpenAI API endpoints are invoked with store: false, preventing your thoughts, transcripts, and entities from being cached or trained on.
